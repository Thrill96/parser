## LAYERED FRAMEWORKS (Apply on top of selected template)

### "Selling to People Above You" (Use when prospect is more successful than Will)
Layer this framework into ANY template when the prospect runs a larger business:
1. Flattery: genuine, specific to their achievements
2. Honest gap: "I'm not in your industry"
3. Smack with ONE inadequacy: "but on this specific metric..."
4. Establish authority: "I built the tool that measures it"

### Contradiction Copy (Use when values conflict with visibility)
Their independence vs being invisible. Their quality vs AI not knowing it. The firms they LEFT now beating them online. The contradiction between WHO they are and HOW the world sees them.

### Conditional Sale (Use for SC and C personality types)
Zero risk. "If this doesn't show you something worth your time, delete it." Removes pressure. Lets the data sell.

## DISC CALIBRATION

### D (Dominant) Prospects
- Short emails. 5 paragraphs max.
- Lead with the bottom line: "You score 29. Here's the fix."
- No buildup. No story. Just the number and the next step.
- Urgency works. "This offer closes Friday."
- CTA: direct. "Call me at [number]."

### I (Influential) Prospects
- Story-driven. Lead with a proof story.
- Social proof matters: "Companies like yours..."
- Name-drop where appropriate.
- Enthusiastic tone. Energy.
- CTA: social. "Let's talk about this."

### S (Steady) Prospects
- No urgency. No scarcity. No pressure.
- Evidence and thoroughness. "I tested this eight different ways."
- Longer email is fine if substantive.
- Reference their stability and track record with respect.
- CTA: low pressure. "Reply with one word whenever you're ready."
- Follow-up: "The data doesn't expire."

### C (Conscientious) Prospects
- Data-heavy. Show the methodology.
- Include specific numbers: scores, percentages, competitor counts.
- They want to verify your claims. Make it easy.
- Reference credentials and frameworks.
- CTA: information-first. "Want me to send the full report with methodology?"

## EMOTIONAL TRIGGER SELECTION

| Trigger | When to Use | Example for AI Outreach |
|---|---|---|
| Fear/Insecurity | Prospect doesn't know they're invisible | "Your competitors show up. You don't." |
| Contradiction | Prospect's values conflict with their reality | "You left Prudential. But Prudential shows up in AI and you don't." |
| Vindication | Prospect left a bigger org to go independent | "The independence you built deserves to be visible." |
| Shame (light) | Prospect is paying for marketing that doesn't work | "The marketing you're paying for has a hole in it." |
| Conditional | Prospect is risk-averse (SC types) | "Delete this if it doesn't show you something new." |
| Greed/Opportunity | Prospect is growth-oriented (DI types) | "Every month at 29/100, that's [X] referrals going to someone else." |

---

## TOUCH SEQUENCE CALIBRATION

### For D/I Prospects (faster decision-makers):
- Touch 1: Day 0
- Touch 2: Day 5 (shorter gap, they decide fast)
- Touch 3: Day 10 (with deadline: "This offer closes [date]")

### For S/C Prospects (slower decision-makers):
- Touch 1: Day 0
- Touch 2: Day 10 (add new value, not pressure)
- Touch 3: Day 20 (respectful close, "the data doesn't expire")
