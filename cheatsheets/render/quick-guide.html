Beginner Tips
What Claude Is

Claude: a family of AI models (Haiku, Sonnet, Opus) that read, write & reason over long text and images.

A thinking partner, not magic — you decide what's right.

It predicts helpful text; it doesn't know you or remember past chats by default.

What It's Great At

Write — emails, posts, resumes, bios.

Summarize — articles, PDFs, threads, transcripts.

Explain — hard topics in plain English.

Plan — trips, meals, projects, launches.

Translate — and shift tone or reading level.

What It Can Read

Attach a PDF, Word doc, spreadsheet, slide deck, or screenshot and ask about it.

Anatomy of a Good Prompt

ROLE — "You are a careful editor."

CONTEXT — who it's for, why, what 'good' looks like.

TASK — one clear thing.

FORMAT — length, tone, table or list.

More of the four = better answers.

+ Task + Format
= a great answer

Talk to It Like a Teammate

Be specific — vague in, vague out.

One task at a time.

Show an example of 'good.'

Ask it to ask you questions first.

Keep refining; it expects edits.

Ask for Format

"As a list / a table / an email / under 100 words."

Watch-Outs

Confident even when wrong — verify facts, names, numbers.

May miss today's news/prices unless it searches.

Each chat starts fresh; re-paste what it needs.

Words to Know

Prompt: your request.

Model: the version doing the thinking.

Token: a chunk of text.

Hallucination: a confident wrong answer.

Context: everything it can see right now.

Stay Safe & Private

Never paste passwords, card numbers, or client secrets.

Strip names before sharing sensitive docs.

Double-check medical, legal & money answers.

Daily Uses

Inbox — draft & soften replies.

Learning — a 5-min explainer + a quiz.

Money — plain-English breakdowns.

Errands — plan, list, compare.

Try This

"You are my AI writing buddy. Ask me 5 questions about my business, then write a warm, confident 3-paragraph About section for my website."

Prompt Patterns That Work

Role — "You are my [expert]."

Recipe — "Go step by step: 1)… 2)…"

Critique & improve — "Critique in 3 bullets, then rewrite."

Format — "Output as a table."

Checklist/SOP — "Turn this into a checklist + SOP."

Question-first — "Ask me 5 questions, then plan."

Few-shot — "Here are 2 examples — match the style."

Get the Tone Right

Name the reader + the feeling.

Paste a voice sample to mimic.

Set the reading level.

Ban clichés and buzzwords.

Ask for Structure

Tables for comparisons.

Numbered steps for how-to.

Checklists for repeatable tasks.

Chain Your Asks
Outline → Draft
→ Critique → Final
beats one mega-prompt
Projects & Artifacts

Projects: persistent workspaces; load docs + a brief once, reused across every chat.

Artifacts: editable docs, tables, code & small apps — not chat bubbles.

Good Projects — course build, client onboarding, SOP library, content calendar.

Work With Long Docs
  1. Paste, then ask ONE question at a time.
  2. Summarize, then list action items + owners.
  3. Pull every date / name / $ into a table.
  4. Ask: what's missing or contradictory?
  5. Order matters:
    1. paste the document FIRST
    2. ask your question LAST
Summarize Anything

Articles, transcripts, threads, PDFs → 5 bullets + next steps.

Research Mode

Search first, paste the findings.

Verify facts with a second source.

Ask it what to search next.

Edit Like a Pro

"Tighten 30%, keep my voice."

"Make line 1 a hook."

"Fix grammar only."

"Flag vague claims; make them specific."

Advanced Tips
The Technique Stack

Chain-of-Thought: "Think step by step" — big jump on multi-step tasks.

Few-shot (2–5): paste input→output pairs; strongest format/voice lever.

Self-consistency: ask 3× in fresh chats, keep the recurring answer.

Self-refine: critique vs a rubric, list flaws, then rewrite.

Tree-of-Thoughts: 3 approaches, score 1–10, expand the winner.

Meta-prompt: write the ideal prompt, then answer it.

Decompose: outline → draft → critique → final.

Advanced Patterns

Least-to-most: solve easy sub-parts first.

Step-back: "What principle governs this?" then answer.

ReAct: reason → search → observe (with tools).

Prompt Add-Ons

"Ask me 3 questions first."

"Show your reasoning briefly."

"Give a confidence note."
