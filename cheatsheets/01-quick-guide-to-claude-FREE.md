## PAGE 1 — BEGINNER BASICS (green tab)

**Header:** WILL STEWART AI · "WORLD'S FRIENDLIEST AI QUICK REFERENCE" · title **Claude — A Quick Reference Guide** · green BEGINNER tab.
**How to use:** Color shows level (green Beginner, amber Intermediate, navy Advanced). Bullets are actions. Tables are for looking up. Cream boxes are examples to copy. No experience needed; each tip stands alone.
**Contents:** Pg.1 Beginner Basics | Pg.2 Core Skills & Patterns | Pg.3 Advanced, Lookups & Daily Use.

**COL 1 — What Claude Is:** an AI you chat with in plain English; you type a prompt, it answers, you refine; it predicts helpful text, doesn't know/remember you; free at claude.ai. **Great at:** writing/editing, summarizing, explaining, brainstorming, planning, fixing code, translating, role-play practice. **Watch for:** confident when wrong (verify facts); may miss today's news/prices; each chat fresh; never paste passwords/cards.
**COL 2 — Set up in 2 min:** free account; newest model; type + Enter; "+" to attach PDF/doc/image; New Chat per topic. **Anatomy of a good prompt:** ROLE, TASK, CONTEXT, FORMAT — more of the four = better. **Talk to it like a teammate:** be specific; one task; show an example; let it ask first; keep refining.
**COL 3 — Your first 8 prompts:** explain like new; rewrite friendly; summarize in 5 bullets; help me reply; 5 ideas for X; simple plan to goal; notes→checklist; quiz me. **Fix a bad answer (table Problem→Say this):** too long→cut in half; too stiff→warmer; too vague→add example; made-up→only facts I gave; wrong shape→table; too generic→specific to me.
**COL 4 — Stay safe & private:** strip names/secrets; double-check medical/legal/money; it can be biased. **Do & Don't (table):** give context / ask for options / split big asks / verify facts. **Words to know:** prompt, model, token, hallucination, context. **TRY THIS box:** "You are a friendly money coach. Explain how a 401(k) works to a new employee in under 120 words, then 3 next steps."

## PAGE 2 — CORE SKILLS & PATTERNS (amber tab)

**COL 1 — Prompt patterns that work:** role play; few-shot (give 2–3 examples); step by step; draft then critique; compare 3 options; template fill. **Get the tone right:** name the reader; name the feeling; give a voice sample; set reading level; ban clichés.
**COL 2 — Work with long text:** paste then ask one Q at a time; summarize→action items; pull dates/names into a table; "what's missing?"; rewrite simpler. **Edit like a pro:** tighten; cut 30%; fix grammar keep voice; hook the first line; vary sentence length.
**COL 3 — Claude vs Search vs People (table):** draft/rewrite→Claude; live facts/prices→Search; today's news→Search; brainstorm→Claude; final medical/legal→a pro; make sense of results→Claude. **Combine tools:** search first then paste; ask what to search next; verify facts with a search.
**COL 4 — Common mistakes→fix (table):** vague→add who+goal; one huge message→split; no example→show one; trust draft 1→ask edits; private data→strip; same prompt twice→tweak one thing. **EXAMPLE BOX — Pattern: Role + Example:** "You are a friendly career coach. Here is a good intro: [paste]. Write 3 more in that style for a [job] seeker."

## PAGE 3 — ADVANCED MOVES & DAILY USE (navy tab)

**COL 1 — Level-up moves:** reuse saved prompts; teach it your voice (paste 2–3 samples); chain outline→draft→edit→final; self-check; use Projects; attach files and ask across them. **Memory & Projects:** a Project keeps context; add brief once; name chats; start fresh on topic change.
**COL 2 — Power prompts (table Want→Try this):** sound like me→match sample; unstuck→5 directions; shorter→cut in half; blind spots→what am I missing; action→checklist; decide→pros/cons + a pick. **Prompt add-ons:** "ask me 3 questions first"; "show reasoning briefly"; "give a confidence note."
**COL 3 — Daily life (table When→Do this):** inbox→draft/soften; learning→explainer+quiz; money→plain breakdowns; errands→plan/compare; writing→outline then draft; tough talk→role-play first. **Weekly habits:** Sun plan; daily summarize+act; Fri draft next week's posts.
