## PANEL 2 — Core Skills (Intermediate / AMBER)

### Prompt Patterns That Actually Work
- **Give it a role:** "You are an expert editor." Sets the tone and skill level.
- **Add context:** who it's for, why it matters, what you already tried.
- **Show an example** of what "good" looks like — Claude matches your sample.
- **Set the format:** "as a table" / "give me 3 options" / "under 100 words."
- **Draft, then refine:** "Make it warmer." "Cut it in half." "More direct."
- **Let it ask first:** "Ask me anything you need before you start."

### When to Use Claude vs. a Search Engine
- Use **Claude** to create, rewrite, explain, or think something through.
- Use **search** for live facts, prices, news, and exact sources.
- Use Claude to make sense of what your search turned up.
- Don't trust Claude for breaking news or anything time-sensitive.

> **Pattern:** Role + Context + Format
> **Template:** "You are a friendly career coach. I'm a [job] applying for [role]. Write 3 opening lines — confident, warm, and bold."
> **Use when:** You want options, not just one answer.

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Too vague → Add who it's for and what "done" looks like.
- One giant ask → Break it into steps, one message at a time.
- Taking the first draft → Ask for changes; that's where it shines.
- Pasting private info → Strip names, account numbers, and secrets first.

## PANEL 3 — Advanced Moves + Lookup (Advanced / NAVY)

### Level-Up Moves
- Save a reusable prompt in a note; paste and tweak it each time.
- Give Claude your voice: paste 2–3 things you wrote so it matches your style.
- Chain steps: outline → draft → edit → final, each in its own message.
- Make it self-check: "What's weak about this answer? Fix it."
- Use Projects (claude.ai) to keep context across a whole task.

### Lookup: Quick Wins
| When you want to… | Try this | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Sound like yourself | "Match the style of this sample: [paste]" | Claude mirrors a tone it can see |
| Get unstuck fast | "Give me 5 directions I could take this" | Options beat a blank page |
| Shorten anything | "Cut this in half, keep the key points" | Forces priority, removes filler |
| Catch blind spots | "What am I missing or assuming here?" | Surfaces gaps you can't see |
| Make it usable | "Format as a checklist I can act on" | Turns advice into action |
