## COL 4 — SETUP + GOVERNANCE + WORKED EXAMPLE
**Set up like an operator:** build 4 Projects (Marketing, Customers, Hiring, Finance/Ops) · load a one-page Business Brief into each · keep a swipe file of prompts that worked · set the redaction rule (what NEVER gets pasted).

## COL 1 — DOCUMENT WORKFLOWS (most underused lever)
**(table — WORKFLOW | OWNER LENS | CLAUDE LENS):**

- Proposals | "Every one looks different." | Turn a winning proposal into a reusable template.

- Grant / RFP | "We miss deadlines or repeat work." | Organize requirements, draft, check alignment.
**Prompt:** *"Summarize key obligations, risks, and any unusual clauses in 10 bullets; flag anything off-market: [paste]."*

## COL 2 — MONEY WORK: FINANCE & CASH FLOW *(connectors: QuickBooks, PayPal)*
- **Cash-flow brief:** Claude reads exported P&L + AR/AP → 1-page owner brief (what changed, risks, actions).
- **Invoice & collections:** drafts a polite→firm late-payment sequence in your voice.
- **Payroll prep:** the finance workflow pulls hours and drafts the run for your approval.
- Pattern: **Data in → Claude does → you decide.** Nothing posts or pays without your click.
**Prompt:** *"Here's our P&L and AR aging [paste]. Write a 1-page brief: top 3 changes, 2 risks, 3 actions I should take this week."*

## COL 3 — REVENUE WORK: MARKETING & SALES *(connectors: HubSpot, Canva)*
- **Campaign builder:** Claude designs an email/social campaign, then drafts assets via Canva.
- **Sales outreach:** builds & personalizes sequences from CRM + enrichment.
- **Meeting prep & follow-up:** summarizes CRM notes; drafts follow-ups in brand voice.
**Framework-powered prompts (the deep stuff):**
- *Hormozi Value Equation:* "Rewrite this offer to maximize (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort). One concrete change per lever."
- *Hormozi Money Model / CFA:* "Map my business into a 4-offer sequence — Attraction, Upsell, Downsell, Continuity. Then audit Client-Financed Acquisition: with CAC [$], 30-day gross profit [$], LTGP [$], is 30-day GP ≥ 2× CAC? Give 3 levers to fix the cash timing." *(The deep, non-obvious panel — see Panel 4 of the landscape build.)*
- *StoryBrand SB7:* "Make the CUSTOMER the hero, us the guide. Output: their want, external/internal/philosophical problem, our 3-step plan, a direct CTA, the stakes of inaction."
