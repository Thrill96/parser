## PANEL 2 — The Owner's Workflow

### Customer Replies — The 6 Messages You Send Every Week
| Situation | Prompt Instruction | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| Unhappy customer | "Draft an empathetic reply that owns the issue and offers a clear fix: [paste]" | Calm, accountable |
| Price pushback | "Help me reframe our price around value, not cost, for: [situation]" | Confident, warm |
| Quote follow-up | "Write a quote follow-up that adds urgency without pressure: [details]" | Friendly, firm |
| Late payment | "Draft a polite but firm payment reminder for [invoice / amount]" | Professional, direct |
| Happy customer | "Turn this thank-you into a review request: [paste]" | Grateful, light |
| Saying no | "Help me decline [request] while keeping the relationship" | Kind, clear |

### Hiring — From Job Post to First Day
**What to feed Claude:** the role, must-have skills, your pay range, your culture in 3 words.

**Prompt template (copy-ready):**
```
Role: You are an expert small-business recruiter.
Task: Write a job post for a [ROLE] at a [INDUSTRY] business with [#] staff.
Details: Must-haves = [SKILLS]. Pay = [RANGE]. Culture = [3 WORDS].
Tone: Real and human, not corporate.
Output: A punchy post under 250 words + 5 screening questions.
```
**Variations:**
- **Speed hire:** add "Also write a 3-line text to send promising applicants today."
- **Vet faster:** "Turn these resumes into a ranked shortlist with one risk flag each: [paste]"

### The Owner's AI-Powered Day
- **Morning (15 min):** "Summarize these overnight emails and flag what only I can answer."
- **Midday:** "Draft replies to the 3 flagged threads in my voice."
- **Marketing block:** "Give me a week of posts from this one idea: [idea]."
- **End of day:** "Turn today's messy notes into tomorrow's task list: [paste]."

### When Claude Gets It Wrong — Fix It Fast
| Problem | What Happened | Fix to Add to Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| Too formal / corporate | Default tone | "Write like a real person talking to a customer" |
| Too long | No length limit | "Keep it under [X] words" |
| Too generic | Missing your context | "We're a [type] business that serves [who]" |
| Wrong reader | No persona given | "The reader is a [specific customer]" |
| Made-up details | No grounding | "Only use the facts I give. Invent nothing." |
| Sounds the same every time | No variety asked for | "Give me 3 versions with different openings" |
