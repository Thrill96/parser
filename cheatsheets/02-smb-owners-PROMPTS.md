[SIENNA HEADER BAR] THE E-MYTH TRUTH (GERBER)
The fatal assumption: being great at the work does NOT mean you can run a business that does that work. Gerber calls the leap "the entrepreneurial seizure."
Three people inside every owner — 1. Entrepreneur (vision, ~10%); 2. Manager (order & systems, ~20%); 3. Technician (does the work, ~70%). Most owners are stuck as the Technician.
Work ON your business, not IN it. Your real product is the BUSINESS, not the service.

Build it as if you'll franchise it (even if you never will). [GREEN BEGINNER]

[SIENNA HEADER BAR] WHERE CLAUDE FITS
You stop being the bottleneck Technician — Claude becomes the system-builder.
It documents what's in your head into SOPs, checklists, and templates anyone can run.
It drafts the offers, emails, and content so growth doesn't depend on your hours.
The limit isn't the tool; it's whether you systematize. Garbage process = garbage output, faster. [AMBER INTERMEDIATE]

COLUMN 2 — render ALL text at 7–8pt:
[SIENNA HEADER BAR] THE VALUE EQUATION (HORMOZI)
Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Success) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice).
Raise the top — paint the dream outcome; prove it's likely (testimonials, guarantees).
Cut the bottom — make it faster and easier than the customer expects.
The Grand Slam Offer: so good people feel stupid saying no — so you stop competing on price.
Enhancers: bonuses, a strong guarantee, honest scarcity & urgency, a magnetic name. [AMBER INTERMEDIATE]

[SIENNA HEADER BAR] CLAUDE PROMPT — REBUILD YOUR OFFER
"You are an offer strategist. Here's my current offer: [paste]. Rewrite it to maximize (Dream Outcome × Likelihood) ÷ (Time × Effort). Make ONE concrete change per lever, add 3 bonus ideas, a risk-reversal guarantee, and 5 names for the offer." [NAVY ADVANCED]

[SIENNA HEADER BAR] THE OWNER'S SCORE
Ask weekly: which tasks only I can do? Which could a system do? Move one task from you → a system every week.

COLUMN 3 — render ALL text at 7–8pt:
